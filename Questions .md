The 10 eval questions

Easy

1. How many customers are there?

2. How many tracks are in the Rock genre?

3. What is the total revenue across all invoices?


Medium

4. What are the top 5 billing countries by total revenue?

5. Which 3 artists have the most albums?

6. What are the top 5 genres by sales revenue? (Use InvoiceLine.UnitPrice * Quantity.)

7. How many invoices were there per year?

8. What is the average invoice total per billing country, only for countries with at least 5 invoices?

Hard

9. Who is the top-spending customer in each country?
